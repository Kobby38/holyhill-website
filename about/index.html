About us

A house of prayer for a city on fire.A house of prayer for a city on fire.

HolyHill began as a prayer meeting and never stopped being one. Today we're a multi-generational, multi-cultural family learning to love God and love our neighbors.

Our story

It started with seven people and a promise.It started with seven people and a promise.

In 2019, seven people began meeting to pray. That prayer meeting, Devoted to Prayer, was the beginning of HolyHill, and it was carried by one cry from Psalm 85:6: “Will you not revive us again?”

  1. 2019

    Devoted to Prayer begins with seven people gathered to pray.

  2. 2023

    Grow in Grace begins, bringing the Word, worship, and prayer every weekday, and Atmosphere of Worship and Fire begins as a monthly night of worship and prayer.

Our vision

To raise a devoted people who know God deeply, walk in His presence, stand faithfully in prayer, and become vessels through whom revival, transformation, and the Kingdom of God are established in their generation.To raise a devoted people who know God deeply, walk in His presence, stand faithfully in prayer, and become vessels through whom revival, transformation, and the Kingdom of God are established in their generation.

Our mission

HolyHill Revival Ministries exists to call people back to the place of prayer and intimacy with God, equipping them through the Word, discipleship, worship, and fellowship to live transformed lives and become carriers of His presence and agents of revival.

  • The Word

    Rooted in the truth of Scripture.

  • Discipleship

    Growing in Christ, walking together.

  • Worship

    Living in His presence and intimacy with God.

  • Fellowship

    One family in Canada and around the world.
