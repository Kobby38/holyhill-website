How it works

Fifteen minutes a day changes a year.Fifteen minutes a day changes a year.

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Let us know you're in so we can cheer you on and add you to the challenge group.

  2. 02

    Read daily

    About 3 to 4 chapters a day, roughly 15 to 20 minutes. The plan below shows each day's reading.

  3. 03

    Share as you go

    Talk about what you're reading with others taking the challenge, and encourage each other to keep going.

  4. 04

    Finish strong

    Missed a day? Don't quit. Pick up at today's reading and catch up when you can.

The plan

Twelve months through the whole Word.Twelve months through the whole Word.
